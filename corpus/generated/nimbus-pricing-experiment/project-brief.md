# Nimbus Pricing Experiment: project brief

Nimbus Pricing Experiment is a Product and Design project at Larkfield. It ran from 2023-03 to 2023-07.
Nimbus Pricing Experiment relies on Prototyping, Usability Testing, Jira, Figma and Amplitude.
Nimbus Pricing Experiment uses Prototyping for reporting.
Nimbus Pricing Experiment uses Usability Testing for day-to-day tracking.
Nimbus Pricing Experiment uses Jira for the main workflow.
Nimbus Pricing Experiment uses Figma for day-to-day tracking.
Nimbus Pricing Experiment uses Amplitude for the final deliverables.
The team is listed in the kickoff email for this project.
