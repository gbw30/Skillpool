# Pioneer Brand Refresh: campaign brief

Pioneer Brand Refresh is a Marketing project at Larkfield. It ran from 2023-01 to 2023-12.
Pioneer Brand Refresh relies on Figma, Google Analytics, Content Strategy and Marketo.
Pioneer Brand Refresh uses Figma for handoffs between teams.
Pioneer Brand Refresh uses Google Analytics for day-to-day tracking.
Pioneer Brand Refresh uses Content Strategy for reporting.
Pioneer Brand Refresh uses Marketo for reporting.
The team is listed in the kickoff email for this project.
